About Us

AgroMarket Rulindo ni urubuga rugamije gufasha abahinzi n’abaguzi kubona isoko ryizewe ry’ibikomoka ku buhinzi bishya kandi byiza.

Dufasha abahinzi bo mu karere ka Rulindo kugurisha umusaruro wabo ku giciro cyiza kandi tugahuza abakiriya n’ibicuruzwa byiza by’umwimerere.

Intego yacu ni uguteza imbere ubuhinzi bugezweho, kongera umusaruro, no korohereza ubucuruzi hagati y’abahinzi n’abaguzi.
